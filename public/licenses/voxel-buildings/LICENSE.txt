The source VOX files under assets/source-buildings are unmodified. Per-file
SHA-256 hashes, archive hash, byte counts, author/source/license records and
transformation details are in source-manifest.json. preview.jpg is the source
pack preview, retained as a reference.

Our importer uses a lossless, palette-uniform greedy merge capped at 8 native
cells on each axis. It preserves every occupied cell and color and every empty
gap. This is not a hand-built substitute, bounding-hull approximation, or
walkable-interior conversion. Most source buildings have solid interiors.
Runtime transforms native Z-up to world Y-up at default scale 0.5 (neighborhood model 6 uses 0.3 and model 7
uses 0.4) and uses each merged
box's actual dimensions. Removing a box removes that exact occupied volume.

Models 8, 9, 10 and 11 have floating details in the original source. Their
unmodified sources and lossless conversions are archived, but only fully
face-connected-to-ground models 1–7, 12 and 13 are currently placed in maps.
